Company Profile

A Tradition of Metallurgical Precision

Established as a premier manufacturing unit in the heart of Rajkot's Rushikesh Industrial Zone, Ghanshyam Technocast (formerly operating under the auspicious name of Shree Gel) has engineered high-integrity steel casting solutions for a decade.

Under the visionary leadership of our founder and Director, Dipak Meghani, the company has integrated a state-of-the-art foundry sand-moulding floor with an advanced CNC and VMC precision machining workshop. This complete vertical integration enables us to deliver ready-to-assemble castings with exact micron tolerances directly to critical industries worldwide.

From custom valve casings and pump impellers to heavy transmission gearbox units, our castings undergo rigorous chemical verification, grain structural testing, and coordinate inspection mapping to meet carbon, alloy, and stainless steel international grade classifications (ASTM, EN, DIN).

Zero Defect Policy

Strict Metallurgical Assurance System

At Ghanshyam Technocast, quality is not a post-production check; it is an active metallurgical design principle. We check each furnace melt iteration before pouring using standard spectral testing devices to match specific ASTM, DIN, and EN alloy grades.

Emission Spectrometry

Checking precise concentration values of Carbon, Nickel, Chromium, and Silicon.

NDT Examination

Ultrasonic and Magnetic Particle Testing to verify zero sub-surface blow-holes or hairline cracks.

GD&T Mapping

Using advanced verniers and gauges to verify dimensional accuracies on VMC profiles.

ISO Compliant Protocols

All metallurgical processes operate under strict, standardized ISO documentation guidelines, guaranteeing trace-coded batches for every component.
