Core Engineering

Our Manufacturing Capabilities

Steel Casting Solutions

High-grade sand casting and investment casting processes optimized for diverse weights and complex geometries. We cast Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, Stainless Steel, and Duplex grades.

  • Weight range: 1kg to 1200kg
  • High structural and mechanical integrity
  • Zero blow-hole guarantee casting tech
  • Complex, thin-walled investment designs

CNC & VMC Machining

Equipped with state-of-the-art CNC Turning Centers and Vertical Machining Centers (VMC) for post-casting secondary operations. We achieve tight micron tolerances directly from rough castings.

  • High-speed indexable turning
  • 3-axis and 4-axis VMC milling
  • Tight geometric tolerances (GD&T)
  • In-house jig and fixture development

Quality Assurance Lab

We perform rigorous tests throughout the production cycle to guarantee zero-defect products. Every batch is certified with complete traceability, including chemical and mechanical analysis certificates.

  • Optical Emission Spectrometer testing
  • Radiography & Ultrasonic inspections
  • Tensile and Hardness testing
  • Coordinate Measuring Machine checks
Machinery Inventory

Advanced Precision Tooling Assets

Our workshop floor in Rajkot is laid out to support batch production runs alongside custom tooling prototyping. We invest in high-speed, robust machining equipment that guarantees dimensional consistency over thousands of cycles.

CNC Lathe Machines

Optimized for cylindrical profile cutting, threading, and face turning of casting blocks.

VMC Milling Systems

Used for complex profiles, hole patterns (PCD layouts), slotting, and accurate surface squaring.

Induction Melting Furnaces

Dual-crucible high-frequency systems allowing rapid alloy smelting with precise temp controls.
